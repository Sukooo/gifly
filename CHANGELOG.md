## 0.1.0 (2026-11-15)

The first release.

- Opens at the cursor with Ctrl+Shift+G, already searching. Enter pastes the GIF into the app you were in, then puts your clipboard back.
- Collections you can drag GIFs onto, reorder, rename and hide, with Ctrl+1 to 9 to jump to one.
- Saves GIF links as you copy them, from Tenor, Giphy, Klipy, Imgur, Twitter (through fxtwitter) and direct GIF links. Any picture link, or image file, can be pasted or dropped into the picker.
- Imports your Discord favorites from the Discord app on your PC, with no token and no network request, or through Discord's API with a token.
- Renews expired Discord links with a bot token.
- Your own GIFs and images, which paste into Discord as attachments.
- Undo for deleting, adding and filing.
- Backups from Settings, with the bot token left out.
- Starts with Windows, waits in the tray using about 2 MB of memory while hidden, and updates itself in the background.
